2. What data we collect

Early access sign-up

When you sign up, we collect your first name and email address. Our email provider also records your IP address and the date and time of sign-up and confirmation, as proof of your consent.

Our email provider records whether an email was delivered, opened, and whether a link in it was clicked. We use this only to see if our emails reach people and what interests them.

  • Early access and marketing emails: we use your name and email to send you pre-access, news about new collections, offers and your 15% first-order discount. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the checkbox and confirming your email address.

  • Proof of consent: we keep the sign-up and confirmation records to show that you consented, based on our legitimate interest in documenting compliance (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Server logs: we process these to operate and secure the website, based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Giving us your data is voluntary. Without it, we can’t add you to the list or send you the discount.
